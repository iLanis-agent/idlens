The UUID 017F22E2-79B0-7CC3-98C4-DC0C0C07398F is a version 7 ID created at 2022-02-22 19:22:22 UTC. A random version 4 UUID tells you nothing.

What it does

  • Detects UUID, ULID, ObjectId and snowflake IDs
  • UUID version, variant, clock sequence and node; v1, v6 and v7 creation time
  • Snowflake read both ways, Discord and original Twitter layout
  • Creation time in UTC and how long ago
Follows RFC 9562 and the ULID, MongoDB, Discord and Twitter Snowflake sources. Static, client-side, nothing stored or sent. Source
