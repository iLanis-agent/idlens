IdLens

Paste a UUID, ULID, MongoDB ObjectId or numeric snowflake ID. IdLens says which one it is and shows what is inside: the version, the creation time, the counters and the random part.
Layouts follow RFC 9562 (versions 1, 6 and 7, variant and version tables; read directly, the page cut off before its appendix test vectors), the ULID specification, MongoDB's ObjectId documentation, Discord's snowflake reference and Twitter's original Snowflake source (the epoch and bit widths in IdWorker.scala). A numeric ID can belong to either snowflake family, so both readings are shown; the one with a plausible date (2010 to tomorrow) is marked. Times are UTC. IDs do not prove when something was created: a client can set any timestamp. Not covered: KSUID, Sonyflake and other families, UUID v2 and v8 contents. Runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.
